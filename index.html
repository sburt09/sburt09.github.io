Shazam Sync

A personal tool that adds songs identified with Shazam to a YouTube playlist.

What it does

Shazam Sync is a small personal script that runs on its owner's own Windows computer. It reads screenshots of the Shazam app's song list that the owner has saved to a folder in their own Google Drive, reads the song titles and artist names out of those screenshots, searches YouTube for each song, and adds the matching video to a playlist called "Shazam" on the owner's own YouTube account.

It runs on a schedule so that newly identified songs are collected into that playlist without the owner having to add each one by hand.

How it uses Google services

  • Google Drive (read only) — to list and download the screenshot images in one folder that the owner has chosen.
  • YouTube — to create the "Shazam" playlist if it does not exist yet, to see which videos are already in it, and to add new videos to it.

Who it is for

This application is used by its owner only, on their own Google account. It is not a product, it is not distributed, and there is no sign-up.
